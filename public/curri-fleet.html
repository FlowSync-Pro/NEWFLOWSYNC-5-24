Strict standards — 3-strike system

To protect our Curri account and every driver on it: unassigning loads, missing pickup/confirmation windows, and freight-securement issues result in strikes. Three strikes in 60 days suspends your access for 90 days. You always see exactly where you stand in your dashboard.

Prior Curri deactivation must be disclosed

Before you accept our invite, tell us if you were ever deactivated on Curri. Their system flags it the moment you accept — undisclosed, that means removal from FlowSync with no refund. Tell us up front and we can usually clear it first.

Questions, answered.

Does it cost anything to apply?▾

No. Applying is 100% free and puts you under no obligation. You only pay the one-time $197 activation after we've reviewed and approved you — and even then you're covered by a 14-day money-back guarantee.

Why can't I just sign up for Curri myself?▾

Actually — you should, and we recommend you apply to Curri directly too. But in most metros Curri's direct signups are closed, with a months-long waiting list. Joining our preferred-carrier fleet does two things while you wait: you start earning Curri money now, and you learn the playbook we've built over years running this fleet — so by the time you get off Curri's list (if ever), you already operate like a pro.

What will it cost once I'm approved?▾

A one-time $197 to activate your fleet spot, then $47/month for membership (starting after your 14-day money-back window). We take no cut of your job pay — you keep 100% of every delivery. No jobs one month? You still just pay the flat $47 — no surprises.

What if I don't get any jobs?▾

Most drivers in busy metros see opportunities within their first week — but volume depends on your vehicle, area, and availability. The dispatcher prioritizes drivers who respond fast and confirm reliably. If it's not working for you in the first 14 days after activating, we refund your $197, no questions asked.

What kind of insurance do I need?▾
  • Under 26 ft (sedan, SUV, pickup, minivan, cargo van, sprinter) — standard personal auto is enough. Liability or full coverage both work. No commercial required.
  • 26-ft box truck — commercial insurance required.
  • Hotshot (truck + trailer over 26 ft) — commercial insurance required.

Worth checking with your insurer before you start: some personal policies exclude "commercial use," meaning a claim could be denied if you're delivering when an accident happens. We don't require commercial under 26 ft, but verifying your policy covers commercial use protects you in the worst case.

How does same-day pay work?▾

Mark a delivery complete before 11 AM Pacific on a business day → your full payout (you keep 100%) is sent the same business day. After 11 AM, or on weekends → next business day. Payouts run through Stripe.

What's the difference between rush and scheduled jobs?▾

Rush jobs need pickup within 30 minutes of being claimed. Scheduled jobs have a future pickup window, but you must confirm 1–3 hours before the scheduled time or you'll be auto-unassigned (a Curri strike). When a job is claimed for you, the notification tells you which type and the deadlines.

Can I cancel the monthly?▾

Anytime. Your $47/month ends at the close of the current billing period — no lock-in. You keep your directory listing and everything from the Driver Pack.

How will I receive my 1099 at tax time?▾

Payouts go through Stripe, which issues you a 1099-K each January if your annual earnings hit the IRS threshold ($2,500 for tax year 2025, $600 for 2026+). You're responsible for reporting all income regardless of whether a form is issued.
