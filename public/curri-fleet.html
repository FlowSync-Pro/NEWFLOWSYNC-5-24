Straight talk

What we promise. What we don't.

What we promise

First-access loads claimed for you, coaching to win with Curri, and payment for every completed job — you keep 95%, same business day if before 11 AM Pacific.

What we don't promise

Any specific volume of jobs, hours, or earnings. Volume varies by your metro, vehicle, availability, and time of day. You're an independent contractor — your own schedule, taxes, vehicle, and insurance.

Strict standards protect everyone

To keep our Curri account in great standing, we hold a 3-strike standard (missed pickup/confirmation windows, unassigning loads, freight issues). You always see exactly where you stand.

Prior Curri deactivation must be disclosed

If you were ever deactivated on Curri, tell us before you accept our invite. Their system flags it the moment you accept — undisclosed, that means removal with no refund. Tell us up front and we can usually clear it first.

Questions, answered.

What happens on the free call?▾
A FlowSync dispatcher calls you back to answer your questions, go over how the fleet works, and confirm it's a fit for your vehicle and area. There's no payment and no pressure — if it's not right for you, no hard feelings.
Do I have to pay to talk to you?▾
No. The call is completely free. You only pay the one-time $97 joining fee if and when you decide to join — and even then you're covered by a 30-day money-back guarantee.
I'm signed up but can't bid on jobs — how does this fix that?▾
As a sub-driver on Curri you can't see or bid on opportunities yourself — only carriers can. Join our fleet and our dispatcher claims jobs on your behalf, matched to your vehicle and schedule. You get the notification; you drive.
What does "preferred carrier" actually mean for me?▾
Years of good standing with Curri make us a preferred carrier — which means we get first access to the highest-value deliveries before they reach the general driver pool. On our fleet, that head start is yours.
Can I use this if I already have my own Curri account?▾
Yes — many drivers double down. Run under our fleet too, and when it genuinely fits without delaying any customer, we coach you on taking a second delivery to make the most of every mile. We show you when it makes sense and when it doesn't.
What does it cost to join?▾
A one-time $97 joining fee, then a flat 5% dispatch fee on jobs you actually complete — you keep 95%. No monthly, ever. No jobs one week? No fee that week.
What kind of insurance do I need?▾
Under 26 ft (sedan, SUV, pickup, minivan, cargo van, sprinter) — standard personal auto is enough. 26-ft box truck or hotshot (truck + trailer over 26 ft) — commercial insurance required.
  • Some personal policies exclude commercial use — worth confirming yours covers it before you start.
How does same-day pay work?▾
Mark a delivery complete before 11 AM Pacific on a business day → your payout (95% of the job) is sent the same business day. After 11 AM, or weekends → next business day. Paid through Stripe.
What if it's not for me?▾
If you join and change your mind, you're covered by a 30-day money-back guarantee on your $97 joining fee — no questions asked. And the call itself is always free.
