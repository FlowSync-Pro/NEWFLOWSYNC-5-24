# Reels / TikTok script pack — FlowSync Drivers

Five short videos, you on camera, phone only. Each one is also an ad creative
for the campaigns in `meta-ad-copy.md` (same rules apply: no income claims,
"one load, not a promise", bids are not pay, prices exactly as the site).

Facts checked against the live site on 2026-10-03: Verified listing $47
one-time with a 30-day no-questions money-back guarantee; Premium $97; fleet
$297 with a 15% dispatching fee on loads, paid every Friday, refundable until
activated. Directory: 232 drivers (check `/find-a-driver` before you say a
number; "over 230" is safe for a few weeks).

---

## How to film (applies to all five)

- **Vertical, 9:16.** Phone at eye level, in the truck or next to it. Daylight.
- **Say the hook in the first 2 seconds.** No "hey guys", no intro, no logo.
- **One idea per video.** 25–45 seconds. Shorter beats longer.
- **Captions on.** Most people watch muted. Instagram/TikTok auto-captions are fine; fix any wrong numbers.
- **On-screen text** is listed per script. Big, 3–6 words, top third of the frame.
- **Screen recordings:** record on your phone (Control Center → Screen Record) and drop them in as cutaways.
- **End every video the same way:** the site name on screen, you say it once.
- **Never show a customer's or driver's name, phone or email** on screen. Blur the bid emails except the dollar amounts.
